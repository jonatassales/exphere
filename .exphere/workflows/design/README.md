# Design — SDD adaptation

Documentary draft. Follows the [common SDD cycle](../README.md) and its [definition](../definition.json) in full. No executor or integration has been implemented.

Adaptations to discuss with design:

- During specification: intent, audience, constraints, and usability and accessibility criteria.
- During execution and verification: evidence from prototype assessment and alignment with the specification.
- During delivery and observation: traceable handoff and feedback on the outcome.
- Artifacts and connections: brief/spec, prototypes, and assessments; design, research, and collaboration tools remain to be defined.

Skills and checkpoints will be domain-specific while preserving common phases, contracts, and traceability.
